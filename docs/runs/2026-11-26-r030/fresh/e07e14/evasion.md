## Held-out scenarios

Written and committed before any fix they test; after the fixes they are run once and never tuned. A held-out scenario counts as detected when any rule fires on it (an alert naming its pod, or an alert about its node with no namespace), because the rule that should catch it may not have existed when it was written.
